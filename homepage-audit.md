# PSDigiLabs Homepage Copy Audit

## 1. Five-second test

**What the homepage currently says:** PSDigiLabs is a product engineering studio that builds Next.js websites and Android apps, with testing and workflow automation as supporting services.

The headline and opening paragraph prioritize platforms and delivery disciplines. A founder or product team looking for design validation before code is unlikely to recognize UI/UX or digital brand design as a service they can buy on its own.

## 2. Gap list

- **The category is engineering-first.** The hero labels the studio an “Independent product engineering studio,” and the headline names Next.js and Android before a buyer problem.
- **Design is not an offer.** UI/UX appears in the founder-background section, not as a named service with a clear scope, deliverables, or standalone entry point.
- **Brand design is absent.** The homepage does not identify digital brand identity or brand application as a capability.
- **No pre-build validation promise.** The process starts with understanding and scoping, but does not explain how research, prototypes, or usability checks can help test an idea before engineering begins.
- **The services hierarchy is unbalanced.** Website development, Android, testing, and automation are explicit. Design is not visible as an equal service.
- **The proof supports delivery more than design.** The three featured projects are framed as live websites and engineering work. They do not show design decisions, validation artifacts, or before-and-after reasoning.
- **The CTA assumes a build project.** “Tell us what you’re building” and “Start a project conversation” make sense for engineering, but do not invite a buyer who is still deciding what to build.
- **The technology list reinforces the current impression.** Next.js, React, Kotlin, Selenium, and automation tools are prominent, while design methods and outputs are not.
- **Search and sharing descriptions repeat the engineering positioning.** The homepage metadata and organization description lead with website development, Next.js, Android, testing, and workflow automation.

## 3. Rewritten homepage outline

### Hero

- **Eyebrow:** UI/UX & Digital Brand Design
- **Headline:** Choose one of the options below.
- **Supporting copy:** Use the paragraph in section 5.
- **Primary CTA:** Discuss a design challenge
- **Secondary CTA:** See selected work
- **Qualification:** State that design engagements can be scoped independently; keep engineering as an optional continuation, not a prerequisite.

### Services

Present design as a standalone capability alongside engineering:

1. **Product strategy & design validation** — clarify users, needs, assumptions, and the riskiest decisions before build.
2. **UI/UX design** — map key journeys, structure information, and create wireframes or interactive prototypes for review.
3. **Digital brand design** — define or refine visual direction and apply it consistently across digital touchpoints.
4. **Design-to-build engineering** — turn an agreed direction into a website or application, with testing and automation available when relevant.

Only list activities PSDigiLabs can consistently deliver; agree on the actual scope and outputs before publishing these service descriptions.

### Process

1. **Frame the decision** — align on the audience, business goal, constraints, and open questions.
2. **Explore and shape** — map journeys and develop the appropriate level of structure, interface, or visual direction.
3. **Validate before build** — review concepts or prototypes with the buyer and, where included in scope, target users.
4. **Handoff or build** — document decisions and deliver design files, specifications, or a separately scoped engineering phase.

Do not imply user research or usability testing is included in every engagement unless that is part of the offer.

### Proof

- Show project snapshots that make the design contribution visible: the brief, a design decision, relevant screens, and the delivered experience.
- Label the studio’s role accurately for each project; separate design work from development work where needed.
- Include testimonials only with publication consent and verified attribution.
- Use verified project outcomes only. Do not imply conversion, revenue, usability, or performance improvements without evidence.

### CTA

Invite design-stage conversations, including projects that are not ready for development. Use the “Design-first” block in section 6.

## 4. Hero headline options

Each option is 12 words or fewer:

1. **Design the right digital product before you build it.** (8 words)
2. **UI/UX and digital brand design, grounded in real product needs.** (10 words)
3. **Validate the experience. Define the brand. Then decide what to build.** (11 words)

## 5. Supporting copy

PSDigiLabs helps founders and product teams make clearer product decisions before code begins. We shape user journeys, interfaces, and digital brand direction, then validate the important assumptions at the right level for the project. Design can be scoped on its own, with engineering available when you are ready to build.

## 6. Design-first CTA block

### Not ready to build yet?

Start with the user experience, the interface, or the digital brand. We’ll clarify what needs to be decided, agree on a focused design scope, and help you assess the direction before committing to development.

**CTA:** Discuss a design challenge

## Claims to verify before publishing

- **“UI/UX & Digital Brand Design” as a service:** confirm which deliverables PSDigiLabs offers directly and can show in a portfolio.
- **Standalone design engagements:** confirm the studio accepts design-only scopes, independently of development.
- **Validation before build:** specify what validation means in practice. User interviews or usability testing require a defined method, participants, and scope; do not imply these are automatically included.
- **Digital brand design:** confirm whether this means visual identity creation, brand refresh, or applying existing brand assets to digital products.
- **Founder experience:** the current homepage cites experience across business analysis, Agile delivery, UI/UX, testing, and engineering. Verify the biography and portfolio substantiate this experience.
- **Three live websites and named clients:** verify project status, the studio’s exact role, and permission to display each client name and work.
- **One-business-day response target:** keep this promise only if it can be consistently met.
- **Worldwide client coverage:** the homepage says the studio works worldwide. Retain only if this reflects actual service availability.
- **Design outcomes:** any claims about improved conversion, usability, engagement, or reduced risk need project-specific evidence and accurate attribution.
